Overlay

Overlay controls the transparent OBS speaker layer. Anchor chooses the screen point the group starts from. Growth decides where new cards are added from that anchor, so a bottom-right overlay can grow left or up instead of covering your scene.

Horizontal shows all currently visible speakers in a row. Vertical stacks speakers in a column. Active only shows one active speaker at a time, which is best when screen space is tight. Persistent keeps user cards visible even when silent, then uses Inactive opacity to dim people who are not talking.

The avatar, name, and test overlay buttons let you preview the OBS look without waiting for a real session. Font Settings tune names and readability. Card Settings tune the bubble shape, color, glow, outlines, and spacing.

Fade milliseconds is visual only. It controls how long speaker cards take to fade, scale, and glow between silent and speaking states. Use 0ms for instant changes, or a higher value for smoother motion. It does not change the audio delay.

Stream Control

Stream Control is the live mix panel for your OBS audio source. Delay shifts the mixed Discord audio so it can line up with camera or capture latency. Turn delay off for non-IRL scenes while keeping the saved delay value ready for later. The small buttons make exact 50ms and 100ms adjustments without fighting the slider on mobile.

User volume changes only your bridge mix. Mute removes a user from your audio mix. Hide removes a user from your overlay. These controls are per bridge profile, so two streamers in the same voice channel can keep different OBS mixes.

The lock buttons are meant for mobile use. Lock a section, or all controls, once the settings are right so accidental taps do not move sliders or reorder sections.
